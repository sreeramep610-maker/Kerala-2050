Digital Heritage Museum

Kerala's living traditions, preserved with immersive technology.

Kathakali

A classical dance-drama known for elaborate makeup, towering costumes and expressive eye and hand gestures (mudras), telling stories from the Ramayana and Mahabharata.

2050 experience: A 3D scanned costume gallery and an AR "become the character" mirror that overlays traditional face makeup in real time.

Classical artAR mirror3D archive

Theyyam

A ritual art form of north Kerala, especially Kannur and Kasaragod, where performers embody deities with vivid costumes, fire and drums.

2050 experience: Volumetric recordings of performances viewable from any angle, with multilingual audio on the stories behind each deity.

Ritual artHolographic storiesOral history

Kalaripayattu

One of the world's oldest martial arts, combining strikes, weapons, healing and discipline, practised in Kerala for centuries.

2050 experience: A VR training dojo with motion tracking that guides beginners through basic stances (meippayattu) with a virtual gurukkal.

Martial artVR dojoMotion capture

Onam

Kerala's harvest festival, celebrated with the pookalam flower carpet, the Onasadya feast, Vallam Kali boat races and Pulikali.

2050 experience: An AI pookalam designer where visitors create a digital flower carpet and see its meaning and history.

FestivalGenerative artFood heritage

Kerala Through Time

From ancient trade routes to a sustainable digital state.

Ancient era

Spice & Sea

The Malabar coast traded pepper, cardamom and cinnamon with the world, and ports like Muziris connected Kerala to distant civilisations.

20th century

Social Progress

Land reforms, near-universal literacy and strong public healthcare shaped the "Kerala Model" of development.

2000s to today
